The mod starts in 527 at the crowning of Justinian I and the breakup of Clovis’ Frankish Kingdom. The mod will cover the transition from Antiquity to the Middle ages.

Thank you Tjrolex, the original developer. 

If this mod crashes, please go to the Imperator folder and delete everything besides the save games and mod folders.
